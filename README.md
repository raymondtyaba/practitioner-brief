# Food Insecurity and Diabetes Practitioner Brief

A healthcare data communication project that translates the relationship between food insecurity and diabetes into a clear, practitioner-focused visual brief.

The project demonstrates how health data and evidence can be presented in an accessible format to support healthcare professionals, community organisations, public health teams, and other non-technical stakeholders.

## Project Overview

Food insecurity can affect a person’s ability to access sufficient, nutritious, and appropriate food. For people living with diabetes, limited access to healthy food may create additional challenges when managing diet, medication, and overall health.

This project communicates the connection between food insecurity and diabetes through a practitioner-ready chart and browser-based brief. The focus is not only on presenting information, but also on making the findings understandable and useful for practical healthcare and public health discussions.

## Project Objectives

The project was developed to:

- Communicate a healthcare issue clearly to practitioner audiences
- Present the relationship between food insecurity and diabetes visually
- Convert analytical information into an accessible professional brief
- Support evidence-informed discussion and decision-making
- Demonstrate responsible communication of health-related information
- Make the final output easy to view and share through a web browser

## Key Features

- **Practitioner-focused communication:** Presents healthcare information in a format suitable for professional audiences.
- **Health data visualisation:** Uses a chart to communicate the relationship between food insecurity and diabetes.
- **Accessible presentation:** Reduces technical complexity and prioritises clear interpretation.
- **Browser-based output:** Allows the brief to be opened without specialist analytical software.
- **Public health relevance:** Connects a social determinant of health with chronic disease management.
- **Portfolio-ready delivery:** Demonstrates the ability to transform healthcare information into a professional digital output.
